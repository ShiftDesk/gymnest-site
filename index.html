Questions

What's in the free plan? +
Up to 20 members, iOS app, check-ins, QR codes, plans, payments, and announcements. Free forever — no time limit, no credit card.
Can I pay from Android or a computer? +
Yes. Subscribe to Pro here on this page from any device. The owner app is iOS only, but the web dashboard at dashboard.gymnest.org works on any browser.
What currencies do you accept? +
135+ currencies via Stripe. Customers in Denmark see DKK, customers in Lebanon see LBP, in the US they see USD — all automatic.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. Cancel anytime from your Stripe billing portal. You keep Pro access until the end of the billing period, then go back to the free plan with 20 members.
Is my payment secure? +
All payments are processed by Stripe — one of the world's most trusted payment platforms. We never see or store your card details.
Do my members pay anything? +
No. GymNest Member is completely free to download and use for your gym members.
